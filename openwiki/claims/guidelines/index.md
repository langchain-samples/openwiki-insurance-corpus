# Files

- [Liability Claim Handling Guidance](liability-claim-handling.md) - Internal workflow for intake, coverage review, investigation, defense, escalation, settlement, recovery, and closure of homeowners liability claims. The applicable policy form, declarations, endorsements, facts, and law control coverage and defense obligations.
- [Mold Claim Handling](mold-claim-handling.md) - Internal guidance for investigating moisture and microbial-loss reports, preserving evidence, directing mitigation, consulting the controlling fungi and water provisions, applying limits, and escalating unresolved or material issues.
- [Roof Claim Handling Guidance](roof-claim-handling.md) - Operational guidance for investigating roof-loss cause, preserving evidence, separating covered damage from condition, valuing the supported scope, escalating uncertainty, communicating the decision, and closing or reopening the claim. It connects claim controls to the applicable roof settlement contract without replacing policy, endorsement, or state requirements.
- [Water Loss Claim Handling](water-loss-handling.md) - Operational workflow for investigating and adjudicating water-loss claims, with edition-specific controls for the HO 04 90 (2026-01) attachment. Separates internal handling practice from policy authority for declarations, sublimits, deductibles, maintenance, backflow prevention, and Coverage C valuation.

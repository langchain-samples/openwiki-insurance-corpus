# Files

- [Claims Manual: Conditions, Authority, and State Operations](conditions-authority-and-state-operations.md) - Operational reference for routing property claims through policy conditions, proof of loss, appraisal, suit, prompt payment, catastrophe, ordinance-or-law, and specialist referral controls. It separates internal handling direction from the policy, endorsement, bulletin, and state-law provisions that control each claim.
- [Claims Intake, Investigation, and Mitigation](intake-investigation-and-mitigation.md) - A contract-aware workflow for receiving a property claim, developing cause and damage evidence, protecting property, and controlling mitigation, coverage, and payment decisions. Internal guidance organizes handling; the in-force policy and attached endorsements control coverage.
- [Claims Manual: Liability, Specialty Property, and Recovery](liability-specialty-and-recovery.md) - Cross-system handling guidance for liability, condominium and loss-assessment, scheduled-property, microbial and roof-related referrals, subrogation, salvage, fraud, and recovery decisions. Use the applicable policy or endorsement for coverage and insured-interest conclusions, and the claims manual for investigation, authority, documentation, referral, and closure controls.
- [Property Perils and Loss Types](property-perils-and-loss-types.md) - Subject-oriented workflows for property claims involving water, roofs, fire, theft, weather, mold, loss of use, and dwelling property. Connects cause, evidence, covered scope, valuation, mitigation, escalation, and consultation of the controlling policy or endorsement.

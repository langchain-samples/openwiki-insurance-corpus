# Water Backup and Sump Discharge Coverage

## Short answer

Water backup is water that backs up through a sewer or drain. Sump discharge or overflow is water discharged or overflowing from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. Coverage depends on the acting base form and attached endorsement: a base-form reference to a backup sublimit is not itself a coverage grant.

The controlling document must be identified first. **HO 04 90 (2026-01) modifies HO-3 Section I—Exclusions A.3 when attached**; its provisions control a conflict, while unmodified policy provisions remain unchanged. It covers direct physical loss to property insured by the HO-3 policy, subject to a **$10,000 policy-period sublimit** (unless Declarations show a higher amount), a separate **$1,000 deductible per loss**, and its conditions. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L1-L4, repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L13-L23, repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L25-L33

## HO-3: HO 04 90 (2026-01)

### Coverage Parts A, B, and C

**HO 04 90 (2026-01) writes back** direct physical loss to property described in Coverage A, Coverage B, and Coverage C when caused by water or waterborne material backing up through sewers or drains, or overflowing or discharging from a sump, sump pump, or related equipment. The endorsement expressly includes such an event “whether or not” it results from mechanical breakdown of the equipment. The cause must still be accidental, and the loss must be direct physical loss during the policy period. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L6-L11, repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L64-L80, repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L82-L95, repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L113-L117

The endorsement's **$10,000 is one policy-period sublimit for all endorsement loss**, not an amount per room, item, insured, or occurrence. It is part of, not in addition to, the Coverage A, B, and C limits. A higher endorsement limit applies only if shown in the Declarations. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L13-L18

A **separate $1,000 deductible applies to each endorsement loss**; the Section I Declarations deductible does not apply to loss covered here. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L20-L23

### Coverage C settlement rule

Property insured under Coverage A and Coverage B is settled under the attached policy's valuation terms. **Coverage C loss is settled at actual cash value**, even if the policy has a replacement-cost personal-property endorsement, unless the Declarations state otherwise for this endorsement. This is a settlement rule, not an expansion of the Coverage C property grant. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L49-L56

### Conditions and below-grade premises

For a residence premises with a finished area below grade, coverage applies only if a backwater valve or equivalent backflow-prevention device was installed and operable on the serving sewer line at the time of loss. The endorsement identifies this as new in 2026-01 and absent from 2010-10. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L42-L47

The insured must use reasonable care to maintain the sump system, promptly repair known damage or malfunction, and keep accessible drains, discharge lines, and related openings free of known obstructions when safely accessible. The endorsement excludes loss caused by failure to maintain when that failure materially contributes to the loss. It also requires mitigation, prompt notice, preservation of evidence, records, photographs, invoices, and access for inspection. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L35-L47, repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L140-L170

### What remains excluded

The write-back is limited; **HO 04 90 (2026-01) preserves the flood and below-ground-water exclusions**. It does not cover flood, surface water not backed up through a sewer or drain, waves, tidal water, storm surge, or overflow of a body of water, **“whether or not driven by wind.”** Section I—Exclusions A.1 continues in full. It also does not cover water below ground that seeps, leaks, flows, or presses through a building, foundation, or swimming pool; Section I—Exclusions A.2 continues in full. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L25-L33

The endorsement separately excludes plumbing-fixture or appliance overflow, certain exterior drainage discharges, materially contributing maintenance failure, and loss consisting of repair or replacement of failed, worn, corroded, deteriorated, or defective equipment. It excludes upgrades or prevention work. Resulting damage may be covered only when the covered water event directly caused direct physical loss. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L119-L146, repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L175-L187

The 2026 wording addresses failure expressly: sump discharge may result from mechanical failure or electrical interruption, and it covers damage when loss of power or mechanical function causes covered water to enter insured property. That does not make the failed pump, system, or repair itself payable. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L93-L95, repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L181-L187, repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L214-L220

## Claim handling checklist

1. Identify the base form, attached endorsement edition, effective date, and Declarations limit.
2. Establish the water path: sewer/drain backup, sump discharge, surface water, flood, below-ground water, fixture/appliance overflow, or another source.
3. Separate resulting direct physical loss from repair, replacement, clearing, maintenance, or upgrade of the failed system.
4. For HO 04 90 (2026-01), test the $10,000 policy-period sublimit, $1,000 separate deductible, Coverage C ACV rule, finished-below-grade valve requirement, and known-maintenance condition.
5. Preserve the failed equipment and evidence where reasonably possible, mitigate further damage, give prompt notice, and provide records and inspection access. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L152-L170

The endorsement's write-back does not erase the preserved flood or below-ground-water exclusions. In particular, the presence of water near a drain or sump does not establish backup; the insured must establish the covered water event and its direct physical loss. Source: repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L175-L179, repo://forms/HO/MS/HO-04-90/2026-01.md#L230-L238
